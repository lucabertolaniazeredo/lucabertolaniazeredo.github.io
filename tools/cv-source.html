Research interests

Political violence, paramilitarism and armed citizenship in Ireland, 1909–1916; the state and private management of violence and policing; youth organisations and the pedagogy of militarism; Italian migration to Ireland from the nineteenth century to the present; comparative and global approaches to social protest and collective violence.
